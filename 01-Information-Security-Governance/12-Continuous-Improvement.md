# Continuous Improvement

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Governance
> **Topic:** Continuous Improvement

## Definition

Continuous Improvement is the ongoing process of evaluating and enhancing the information security program to adapt to changing business needs, risks, technologies, and regulations.

## Why It Matters

Threats, business objectives, and compliance requirements constantly change. Regular reviews ensure the security program remains effective and aligned with organizational goals.

## CISM Key Points

- Review the security program regularly.
- Learn from audits, incidents, and assessments.
- Update policies and procedures as needed.
- Track improvements using metrics and feedback.
- Continuous improvement supports business resilience.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP discusses continuous monitoring and improvement. CISM focuses on improving the entire security governance and management program to better support the business.

## Exam Tip

Security governance is never "finished." The best answer usually involves reviewing results, measuring effectiveness, and improving the program.

## Real-World Example

Following an annual security assessment, an organization updates its policies, improves employee awareness training, and enhances its incident response process to address identified weaknesses.
