# Domain 1 Summary – Information Security Governance

## Goal of Domain 1

Information Security Governance ensures that information security supports business objectives, manages risk, and delivers value to the organization.

## Topics to Remember

### 1. Information Security Governance
- Aligns security with business objectives.
- Provides strategic direction.
- Focuses on accountability and oversight.

### 2. Governance vs. Management
- Governance = What should be done?
- Management = How should it be done?

### 3. Corporate Governance
- Overall governance of the organization.
- Security governance is part of corporate governance.

### 4. Organizational Governance
- Leadership, accountability, policies, and decision-making.
- Supports business success.

### 5. Business Objectives
- Security exists to support business goals.
- Always think business first.

### 6. Business Alignment
- Align security initiatives with organizational strategy.
- Security should enable the business.

### 7. Information Security Strategy
- Long-term roadmap.
- Supports business strategy and risk appetite.

### 8. Policies, Standards, Procedures & Guidelines

Remember the hierarchy:

Policy
→ Standard
→ Procedure
→ Guideline

### 9. Roles & Responsibilities

Board
- Governance

Executive Management
- Direction and resources

Security Manager
- Executes the security program

Employees
- Follow policies

### 10. Governance Frameworks

Know what each one does.

- COBIT → Governance
- ISO 27001 → ISMS
- ISO 27002 → Security Controls
- NIST CSF → Cybersecurity Risk

### 11. Metrics & Reporting

Report information that helps executives make business decisions.

Remember:

- KPI = Performance
- KRI = Risk

### 12. Continuous Improvement

Always:

Assess
→ Improve
→ Measure
→ Repeat

# Top CISM Mindset

When answering exam questions ask yourself:

- Does this support business objectives?
- Does this align with governance?
- Does this manage risk?
- Does this provide value to the organization?
- Is this what executive management would do?

If yes...

...it is probably the correct answer.

---

# CISSP vs CISM

CISSP asks:

"How do I secure it?"

CISM asks:

"How do I manage security to support the business?"

That mindset difference is the key to passing CISM.

## Domain 1 Quick Review Checklist

✓ Governance supports business objectives.

✓ Governance ≠ Management.

✓ Business comes before technology.

✓ Security enables the business.
