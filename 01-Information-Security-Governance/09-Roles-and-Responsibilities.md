# Roles and Responsibilities

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Governance
> **Topic:** Roles and Responsibilities

## Definition

Information security roles and responsibilities define who is accountable for governance, management, implementation, and oversight of the organization's security program.

## Why It Matters

Clearly defined responsibilities improve accountability, reduce confusion, and ensure security decisions are made by the appropriate individuals.

## CISM Key Points

- The Board provides governance and oversight.
- Executive Management implements business and security strategy.
- The Information Security Manager develops and manages the security program.
- System Owners are responsible for protecting their systems and data.
- Every employee shares responsibility for information security.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP explains security roles. CISM emphasizes accountability and ensuring responsibilities support governance and business objectives.

## Exam Tip

Know the difference between **governance (Board)** and **management (Executive Management and Security Manager)**.

## Real-World Example

The board approves the organization's security strategy. Executive management provides funding, while the Information Security Manager implements the security program and reports progress through established governance channels.
