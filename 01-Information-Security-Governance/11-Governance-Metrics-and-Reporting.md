# Governance Metrics and Reporting

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Governance
> **Topic:** Governance Metrics and Reporting

## Definition

Governance metrics measure how effectively the information security program supports business objectives. Reporting communicates these results to executive management and the board.

## Why It Matters

Meaningful metrics help leadership make informed decisions, allocate resources, monitor risk, and determine whether security objectives are being achieved.

## CISM Key Points

- Metrics should support business objectives.
- Report meaningful information, not just technical data.
- Use Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to measure performance.
- Use Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) to monitor risk.
- Reports should help management make decisions.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP introduces security metrics. CISM emphasizes reporting metrics that executives and the board can use for governance and business decisions.

## Exam Tip

Management wants information that supports decision-making—not technical details like firewall logs or vulnerability counts without business context.

## Real-World Example

The security manager reports quarterly KPIs such as phishing success rate, incident response time, and compliance status, allowing executives to evaluate security performance and business risk.
