# Governance Frameworks

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Governance
> **Topic:** Governance Frameworks

## Definition

Governance frameworks provide structured guidance for establishing, managing, and improving an organization's information security governance program.

## Why It Matters

Frameworks help organizations adopt proven practices, improve consistency, manage risk, and demonstrate compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.

## CISM Key Points

- COBIT focuses on governance and management of enterprise IT.
- ISO/IEC 27001 provides requirements for an Information Security Management System (ISMS).
- ISO/IEC 27002 provides security control guidance.
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) helps organizations manage cybersecurity risk.
- Organizations may use one or multiple frameworks based on business needs.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP introduces several frameworks. CISM emphasizes selecting and applying frameworks to support governance and business objectives.

## Exam Tip

Know the primary purpose of each framework rather than memorizing every detail.

## Real-World Example

A healthcare organization adopts ISO/IEC 27001 to build its ISMS while using the NIST CSF to improve cybersecurity risk management and COBIT to strengthen governance.
