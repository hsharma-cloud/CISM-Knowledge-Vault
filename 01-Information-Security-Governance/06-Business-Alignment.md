# Business Alignment

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Governance
> **Topic:** Business Alignment

## Definition

Business Alignment ensures that information security supports the organization's mission, strategic goals, and business priorities. Security should enable the business, not become an obstacle.

## Why It Matters

Security investments should provide business value by protecting critical assets, reducing risk, and supporting organizational objectives. A security program that is not aligned with the business can waste resources and fail to address the organization's highest priorities.

## CISM Key Points

- Security must support business objectives.
- Align security initiatives with organizational strategy.
- Understand business priorities before recommending security solutions.
- Communicate with business stakeholders regularly.
- Measure security success by its contribution to business goals.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP emphasizes protecting information assets. CISM goes further by asking how security enables the business and supports organizational success.

## Exam Tip

On the CISM exam, the correct answer is often the one that best aligns security with business objectives—not necessarily the most technically secure option.

## Real-World Example

A company is expanding into cloud services. Instead of applying every possible security control, the security manager prioritizes controls that protect customer data, meet compliance requirements, and support the company's growth strategy.
