# Organizational Governance

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Governance
> **Topic:** Organizational Governance

## Definition

Organizational Governance is the system of leadership, policies, processes, and controls that directs an organization toward achieving its objectives while balancing risk, compliance, and stakeholder expectations.

## Why It Matters

Effective organizational governance ensures that business goals, risk management, and information security work together. It provides accountability, supports ethical decision-making, and helps organizations achieve long-term success.

## CISM Key Points

- Organizational governance aligns business objectives with organizational activities.
- It defines accountability and decision-making authority.
- Information Security Governance is part of Organizational Governance.
- Executive leadership establishes direction and oversight.
- Security initiatives should always support business objectives.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP introduces governance concepts, but CISM focuses on how governance enables business success and how managers support organizational objectives.

## Exam Tip

For CISM questions, always choose the answer that best aligns security with business goals and organizational governance—not the most technical solution.

## Real-World Example

A company's executive leadership establishes a governance framework requiring all business units to assess cyber risk before launching new projects. The information security manager ensures security reviews are completed and reports risks to leadership before approval.
