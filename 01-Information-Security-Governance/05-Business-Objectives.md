# Business Objectives

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Governance
> **Topic:** Business Objectives

## Definition

Business objectives are the measurable goals an organization wants to achieve. Information security should support these objectives rather than operate independently.

## Why It Matters

Security exists to enable the business. Every security initiative should help the organization achieve its goals while managing risk at an acceptable level.

## CISM Key Points

- Business objectives drive security priorities.
- Security must support—not hinder—the business.
- Resources should be allocated based on business value and risk.
- Security decisions should align with organizational strategy.
- Executive management expects security to enable business success.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP discusses business requirements, but CISM places much greater emphasis on aligning every security decision with business objectives.

## Exam Tip

If two answers are technically correct, choose the one that best supports the organization's business objectives.

## Real-World Example

A company plans to expand into online sales. The security team prioritizes securing the e-commerce platform because it directly supports the organization's strategic business objective.
