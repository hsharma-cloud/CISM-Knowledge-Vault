# Information Security Strategy

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Governance
> **Topic:** Information Security Strategy

## Definition

An Information Security Strategy is a long-term plan that aligns security objectives with the organization's business goals and risk appetite.

## Why It Matters

A well-defined strategy ensures that security investments support business priorities, manage risk effectively, and provide measurable value to the organization.

## CISM Key Points

- Align security strategy with business strategy.
- Support the organization's mission and objectives.
- Consider risk appetite and available resources.
- Review and update the strategy regularly.
- Measure progress using meaningful metrics.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP focuses on designing and implementing security controls. CISM focuses on creating a strategy that guides those controls to support business objectives.

## Exam Tip

Always choose the answer that aligns the security strategy with business strategy rather than implementing isolated technical solutions.

## Real-World Example

A financial institution develops a three-year security strategy that prioritizes cloud security, regulatory compliance, and employee awareness training to support its digital transformation goals.
