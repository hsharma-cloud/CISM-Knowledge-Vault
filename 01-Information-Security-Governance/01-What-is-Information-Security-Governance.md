# What is Information Security Governance?

## Purpose
Explain the purpose of Information Security Governance and how it enables the organization to achieve its business objectives through effective direction, oversight, and accountability.
## Learning Objectives
- Understand the purpose of Information Security Governance.
- Explain how governance supports business objectives.
- Identify the key components of an effective governance framework.
- Describe the roles and responsibilities of senior management and the board of directors in information security governance.

## Introduction
Information Security Governance provides the strategic direction and oversight necessary to ensure that information security supports the organization's mission, business objectives, and risk management strategy.

## Why It Matters
Without effective governance, information security initiatives may not align with business priorities, resources may be used inefficiently, and risks may not be managed according to the organization's objectives and risk appetite.
## Key Concepts
- Governance establishes direction and accountability.
- Information security must support business objectives.
- Executive management and the board provide oversight.
- Policies, standards, and risk management are governed at the organizational level.


## Real-World Example
A healthcare organization establishes an Information Security Governance Committee composed of executives from IT, legal, compliance, finance, and business operations. The committee meets regularly to review security risks, approve policies, allocate resources, and ensure that security initiatives support the organization's strategic goals and regulatory requirements.
## Manager's Perspective
An information security manager focuses on ensuring that security initiatives support business objectives, risks are managed within the organization's risk appetite, resources are used effectively, and governance decisions are communicated to executive leadership. The manager is responsible for enabling informed decision-making rather than implementing every technical control.
## Key Takeaways
- Information Security Governance aligns security with business objectives.
- Governance provides strategic direction, oversight, and accountability.
- Executive leadership and the board play a critical governance role.
- Effective governance enables informed risk-based decision-making.
- Governance focuses on *what* should be achieved, while management focuses on *how* to achieve it.
