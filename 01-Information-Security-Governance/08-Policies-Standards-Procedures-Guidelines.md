# Policies, Standards, Procedures, and Guidelines

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Governance
> **Topic:** Policies, Standards, Procedures, and Guidelines

## Definition

These governance documents establish how an organization manages information security. They provide direction, define mandatory requirements, describe implementation steps, and recommend best practices.

## Why It Matters

A clear documentation hierarchy ensures consistency, accountability, compliance, and effective security operations across the organization.

## CISM Key Points

- **Policy:** High-level management statement of intent.
- **Standard:** Mandatory requirements supporting a policy.
- **Procedure:** Step-by-step instructions for performing a task.
- **Guideline:** Recommended, non-mandatory best practices.
- Policies are approved by senior management.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP introduces these documents, while CISM emphasizes their role in governance and ensuring organizational consistency.

## Exam Tip

Remember the hierarchy:

**Policy → Standard → Procedure → Guideline**

## Real-World Example

A company publishes an Information Security Policy requiring multi-factor authentication. A standard specifies approved MFA methods, procedures explain enrollment steps, and guidelines recommend securing personal devices.
