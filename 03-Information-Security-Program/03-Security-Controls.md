# Security Controls

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Program
> **Topic:** Security Controls

## Definition

Security controls are safeguards implemented to reduce risk and protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets.

## Why It Matters

Controls reduce business risk and help the organization achieve its security and compliance objectives.

## CISM Key Points

- Controls should be risk-based.
- Select controls that support business objectives.
- Administrative, Technical, and Physical controls.
- Preventive, Detective, Corrective, Deterrent, Compensating, and Recovery controls.
- Regularly review control effectiveness.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP explains how controls work. CISM focuses on selecting and managing controls that provide the greatest business value.

## Exam Tip

The best control is the one that appropriately reduces business risk—not necessarily the strongest or most expensive.

## Real-World Example

A company implements Multi-Factor Authentication to reduce the risk of unauthorized access while maintaining usability for employees.
