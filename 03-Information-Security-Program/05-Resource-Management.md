# Resource Management

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Program
> **Topic:** Resource Management

## Definition

Resource Management ensures that people, budget, technology, and time are effectively allocated to support the information security program.

## Why It Matters

Resources are limited. Security managers must prioritize investments that provide the greatest value and reduce the most significant business risks.

## CISM Key Points

- Allocate resources based on risk.
- Balance cost and business value.
- Ensure qualified personnel.
- Prioritize high-risk initiatives.
- Review resource needs regularly.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP discusses security controls. CISM focuses on managing resources to achieve business and security objectives.

## Exam Tip

When resources are limited, prioritize initiatives that reduce the highest business risk.

## Real-World Example

A security manager delays a low-risk project and assigns staff to strengthen ransomware protection because it poses a greater risk to the organization.
