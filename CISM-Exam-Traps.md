# CISM Exam Traps (CISSP → CISM Bridge)

## Purpose

This guide highlights common ISACA exam patterns and helps shift your mindset from technical implementation (CISSP) to security management (CISM).

---

# Rule #1

Business before technology.

If two answers are technically correct, choose the one that best supports business objectives.

---

# Rule #2

Governance before management.

Governance sets direction.

Management executes.

---

# Rule #3

Business owns the risk.

Security identifies and recommends.

Business management accepts or rejects the risk.

Never let the security manager own business risk.

---

# Rule #4

Risk cannot be eliminated.

The goal is acceptable risk.

---

# Rule #5

Follow the process.

Don't skip steps.

ISACA likes structured decision making.

---

# Rule #6

Policies before technology.

Always verify:

- Policy
- Standard
- Procedure

before implementing technical controls.

---

# Rule #7

Communicate first.

For major incidents:

- Notify management.
- Follow the communication plan.
- Escalate appropriately.

---

# Rule #8

Senior management approves.

Security managers recommend.

Executives approve.

The Board provides oversight.

---

# Rule #9

Think strategically.

Avoid answers focused on a single server, firewall, or technology.

Think enterprise-wide.

---

# Rule #10

Measure business value.

Metrics should answer:

"How is security helping the business?"

Not

"How many alerts did the firewall generate?"

---

# Rule #11

Continuous improvement.

After every:

- Audit
- Incident
- Assessment
- Review

Improve the program.

---

# Rule #12

People before technology.

Training.

Awareness.

Communication.

Governance.

These often beat buying another security tool.

---

# Rule #13

Risk-based decisions.

Never implement expensive controls without understanding:

- Business impact
- Risk
- Cost
- Benefit

---

# Rule #14

Business Continuity ≠ Disaster Recovery

Business Continuity

Keeps the business operating.

Disaster Recovery

Restores IT.

---

# Rule #15

Protect evidence.

During incidents:

Preserve evidence.

Maintain chain of custody.

Follow procedures.

---

# Rule #16

Third-party risk remains your risk.

Outsourcing services does not outsource accountability.

---

# Rule #17

Alignment is everything.

Almost every correct answer supports:

- Business objectives
- Governance
- Risk management
- Business value

---

# 10 Questions to Ask Yourself

Before selecting an answer ask:

1. Does it support the business?

2. Is it risk-based?

3. Is governance involved?

4. Is the process being followed?

5. Who owns the risk?

6. Has management been informed?

7. Is this strategic?

8. Is this improving the program?

9. Is communication appropriate?

10. Is this what a manager would do?

---

# Final Mindset

CISSP says:

Protect the systems.

CISM says:

Protect the business.

Never forget that difference.
