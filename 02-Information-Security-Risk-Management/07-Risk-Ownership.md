# Risk Ownership

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Risk Management
> **Topic:** Risk Ownership

## Definition

Risk Ownership is the assignment of responsibility for making decisions about identified risks. The risk owner is accountable for accepting, mitigating, transferring, or avoiding the risk.

## Why It Matters

The information security manager identifies and communicates risks, but the business owner is responsible for deciding how the risk will be handled.

## CISM Key Points

- Risk owners are business owners.
- Security managers advise; they do not own business risk.
- Risk decisions should align with business objectives.
- Risk ownership must be clearly assigned.
- Accountability is essential.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP discusses risk ownership. CISM strongly emphasizes that business management—not IT or security—owns business risk.

## Exam Tip

If asked who accepts a risk, the answer is the **risk owner (business owner)**, not the security manager.

## Real-World Example

The owner of an online payment application accepts the residual risk after reviewing the security team's assessment and recommendations.
