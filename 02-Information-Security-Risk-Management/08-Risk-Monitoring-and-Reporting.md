# Risk Monitoring & Reporting

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Risk Management
> **Topic:** Risk Monitoring & Reporting

## Definition

Risk Monitoring is the continuous process of tracking risks and control effectiveness. Risk Reporting communicates risk information to management for informed decision-making.

## Why It Matters

Risks change over time. Continuous monitoring helps management respond to new threats and ensures security remains aligned with business objectives.

## CISM Key Points

- Monitor changes in risk.
- Review the effectiveness of controls.
- Report significant risks to management.
- Use KPIs and KRIs to support decisions.
- Update the risk register regularly.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP focuses on continuous monitoring. CISM emphasizes reporting risk information that supports business and executive decisions.

## Exam Tip

Management needs meaningful risk information—not technical details.

## Real-World Example

The security manager provides a quarterly risk report highlighting high-risk issues, control effectiveness, and recommended actions for executive review.
