# Risk Treatment

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Risk Management
> **Topic:** Risk Treatment

## Definition

Risk Treatment is the process of selecting and implementing the appropriate response to a risk based on business objectives and risk appetite.

## Why It Matters

Not every risk should be eliminated. Management chooses the most appropriate treatment based on business value, cost, and acceptable risk.

## CISM Key Points

- Four treatment options:
  - Accept
  - Mitigate
  - Transfer
  - Avoid
- Select the option that best supports the business.
- Consider cost versus benefit.
- Risk decisions should align with risk appetite.
- Residual risk may remain after treatment.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP explains risk response methods. CISM emphasizes choosing the treatment that best supports business objectives.

## Exam Tip

Eliminating every risk is rarely the correct answer. Choose the treatment that provides the best business value.

## Real-World Example

A company purchases cyber insurance to transfer part of its financial risk while implementing additional security controls to reduce the remaining risk.
