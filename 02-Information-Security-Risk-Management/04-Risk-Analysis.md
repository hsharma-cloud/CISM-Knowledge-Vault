# Risk Analysis

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Risk Management
> **Topic:** Risk Analysis

## Definition

Risk Analysis is the process of understanding the nature of a risk by evaluating its likelihood, impact, existing controls, and potential effect on business objectives.

## Why It Matters

Risk analysis helps management determine which risks require immediate attention and which can be accepted, transferred, mitigated, or avoided.

## CISM Key Points

- Analyze likelihood and business impact.
- Consider existing security controls.
- Evaluate potential business consequences.
- Support risk-based decision making.
- Document results in the risk register.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP explains the mechanics of risk analysis. CISM focuses on using the results to support management decisions and business priorities.

## Exam Tip

The purpose of risk analysis is to help management make informed business decisions—not to eliminate every risk.

## Real-World Example

An organization analyzes the risk of storing customer data in the cloud by evaluating security controls, compliance requirements, and the potential business impact of a data breach.
