# Third-Party Risk Management

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Risk Management
> **Topic:** Third-Party Risk Management

## Definition

Third-Party Risk Management is the process of identifying, assessing, monitoring, and managing risks introduced by vendors, suppliers, contractors, and service providers.

## Why It Matters

Organizations remain responsible for protecting their information even when services are outsourced. Third-party failures can impact security, compliance, and business operations.

## CISM Key Points

- Assess vendors before engagement.
- Include security requirements in contracts.
- Monitor vendor performance regularly.
- Review compliance with agreed controls.
- Third-party risk is an ongoing process.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP discusses supply chain security. CISM focuses on managing third-party risk from a business and governance perspective.

## Exam Tip

Outsourcing a service does **not** outsource accountability. The organization remains responsible for managing the risk.

## Real-World Example

Before moving customer data to a cloud provider, the organization evaluates the provider's security controls, compliance certifications, and contractual obligations.
