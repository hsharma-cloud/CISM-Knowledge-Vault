# Risk Identification

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Risk Management
> **Topic:** Risk Identification

## Definition

Risk Identification is the process of identifying assets, threats, vulnerabilities, and events that could affect the organization's ability to achieve its business objectives.

## Why It Matters

You cannot manage a risk until you know it exists. Identifying risks early allows the organization to make informed business decisions.

## CISM Key Points

- Identify critical business assets.
- Identify threats and vulnerabilities.
- Consider internal and external risks.
- Involve business stakeholders.
- Keep the risk register current.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP focuses on identifying threats and vulnerabilities. CISM emphasizes identifying risks that impact business objectives.

## Exam Tip

Business impact comes first. Focus on risks that could affect the organization's mission and objectives.

## Real-World Example

Before launching a new customer portal, the organization identifies risks such as data breaches, service outages, and regulatory compliance issues.
