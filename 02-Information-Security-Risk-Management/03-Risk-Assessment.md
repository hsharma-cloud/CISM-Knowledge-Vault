# Risk Assessment

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Risk Management
> **Topic:** Risk Assessment

## Definition

Risk Assessment is the process of evaluating identified risks by analyzing their likelihood and business impact to determine their priority.

## Why It Matters

Organizations have limited resources. Risk assessment helps management focus on the risks that matter most to the business.

## CISM Key Points

- Evaluate likelihood and impact.
- Prioritize risks based on business impact.
- Consider existing controls.
- Use qualitative or quantitative methods.
- Reassess risks regularly.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP explains how to assess risk. CISM focuses on using the assessment to support business decisions and resource allocation.

## Exam Tip

The highest priority is the risk with the greatest business impact—not necessarily the most technical vulnerability.

## Real-World Example

A ransomware attack on a payment system is rated higher than a minor website issue because it would significantly disrupt business operations.
