# Domain 2 Summary – Information Security Risk Management

## Goal of Domain 2

Manage information security risk to support business objectives and enable informed business decisions.

---

---

## Topics to Remember

### 1. Risk Management Fundamentals
- Risk cannot be eliminated.
- Risk should be managed.
- Support business objectives.

### 2. Risk Identification
- Identify assets.
- Identify threats.
- Identify vulnerabilities.
- Update the risk register.

### 3. Risk Assessment
- Measure likelihood.
- Measure business impact.
- Prioritize risks.

### 4. Risk Analysis
- Understand business consequences.
- Consider existing controls.
- Support management decisions.

### 5. Risk Treatment

Four options:

- Accept
- Mitigate
- Transfer
- Avoid

### 6. Risk Appetite, Tolerance & Capacity

- Appetite = Willing to accept
- Tolerance = Acceptable variation
- Capacity = Maximum possible risk

### 7. Risk Ownership

Remember:

**Business owns the risk.**

Security advises.

Management decides.

### 8. Risk Monitoring & Reporting

- Monitor continuously.
- Report meaningful information.
- Help management make decisions.

### 9. Third-Party Risk

- Assess vendors.
- Monitor vendors.
- Organization remains accountable.

### 10. Business Impact Analysis

- Identify critical business processes.
- Determine recovery priorities.
- Focus on business impact.

---

# Top CISM Mindset

Always ask:

- Does this support business objectives?
- Who owns the risk?
- Is the risk within appetite?
- Has management been informed?
- What provides the best business value?

---

# CISSP vs CISM

CISSP asks:

"How do I reduce the risk?"

CISM asks:

"What is the best business decision regarding this risk?"

---

## Domain 2 Quick Review Checklist

✓ Business owns risk.

✓ Risk cannot be eliminated.

✓ Choose the treatment that provides the best business value.

✓ Think like a manager—not an engineer.
