# Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Risk Management
> **Topic:** Business Impact Analysis

## Definition

A Business Impact Analysis (BIA) identifies critical business processes and evaluates the impact of disruptions on the organization.

## Why It Matters

A BIA helps management prioritize recovery efforts, allocate resources, and support business continuity planning.

## CISM Key Points

- Identifies critical business processes.
- Measures business impact of disruptions.
- Determines recovery priorities.
- Supports Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery planning.
- Focuses on business impact—not technical failures.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP explains how to perform a BIA. CISM emphasizes using BIA results to support business decisions and prioritize resources.

## Exam Tip

A BIA identifies **business impact and recovery priorities**. It does **not** identify threats or vulnerabilities.

## Real-World Example

A hospital performs a BIA and determines that electronic medical records must be restored within two hours because patient care depends on them.
