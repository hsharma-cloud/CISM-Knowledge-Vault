# Risk Management Fundamentals

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Risk Management
> **Topic:** Risk Management Fundamentals

## Definition

Information Security Risk Management is the process of identifying, assessing, treating, monitoring, and communicating information security risks to support business objectives.

## Why It Matters

Risk cannot be eliminated—only managed. The goal is to reduce risk to a level acceptable to the organization while enabling the business.

## CISM Key Points

- Risk is part of every business.
- Risk management supports business objectives.
- Not all risks should be eliminated.
- Decisions should align with the organization's risk appetite.
- Risk management is a continuous process.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP teaches risk concepts. CISM focuses on making risk-based business decisions and communicating them to management.

## Exam Tip

Choose the answer that balances business objectives with acceptable risk rather than eliminating all risk.

## Real-World Example

A company accepts the low risk of a non-critical vulnerability because the cost of fixing it exceeds the business benefit.
