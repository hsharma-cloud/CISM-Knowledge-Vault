# Risk Appetite, Risk Tolerance & Risk Capacity

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Information Security Risk Management
> **Topic:** Risk Appetite, Risk Tolerance & Risk Capacity

## Definition

Risk Appetite is the amount of risk an organization is willing to accept. Risk Tolerance defines the acceptable variation from that appetite. Risk Capacity is the maximum amount of risk the organization can absorb.

## Why It Matters

These concepts help management make consistent risk decisions that support business objectives without exposing the organization to unacceptable risk.

## CISM Key Points

- Risk Appetite = Willing to accept.
- Risk Tolerance = Acceptable variation.
- Risk Capacity = Maximum possible risk before serious impact.
- Established by senior management and the board.
- Guides security and business decisions.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP introduces these concepts. CISM expects you to apply them when making business and governance decisions.

## Exam Tip

Always make risk decisions within the organization's approved risk appetite.

## Real-World Example

A bank has a very low risk appetite for customer data breaches but a higher appetite for adopting new technology that improves customer experience.
