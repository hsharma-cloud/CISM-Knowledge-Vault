> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04

> **Topic:** Incident Management Fundamentals

## Definition

Incident Management is the process of preparing for, detecting, responding to, recovering from, and learning from information security incidents.

## Why It Matters

Effective incident management minimizes business disruption, reduces financial loss, and improves the organization's resilience.

## CISM Key Points

- Prepare before incidents occur.
- Detect incidents quickly.
- Respond to contain damage.
- Recover normal operations.
- Learn from every incident.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP explains incident response procedures. CISM focuses on managing the incident response process to reduce business impact.

## Exam Tip

The objective is to restore business operations safely while minimizing risk and preserving evidence.

## Real-World Example

A ransomware attack is detected. The incident response team isolates affected systems, restores critical services from backups, and conducts a lessons-learned review.
