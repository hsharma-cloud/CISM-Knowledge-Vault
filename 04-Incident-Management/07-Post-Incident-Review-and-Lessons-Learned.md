# Post-Incident Review & Lessons Learned

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Incident Management
> **Topic:** Post-Incident Review & Lessons Learned

## Definition

A Post-Incident Review evaluates how an incident was handled, identifies what worked well, what did not, and recommends improvements to strengthen the organization's security program.

## Why It Matters

Every incident is an opportunity to improve incident response, reduce future risk, and strengthen business resilience.

## CISM Key Points

- Conduct a lessons learned meeting.
- Identify root causes.
- Evaluate the response process.
- Update policies, procedures, and controls.
- Track improvement actions.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP discusses lessons learned. CISM emphasizes using post-incident reviews to improve governance, risk management, and the security program.

## Exam Tip

The incident is not complete until lessons learned have been documented and improvements have been implemented.

## Real-World Example

After a phishing attack, the organization updates email filtering, improves employee awareness training, and revises the incident response procedure to reduce future incidents.
