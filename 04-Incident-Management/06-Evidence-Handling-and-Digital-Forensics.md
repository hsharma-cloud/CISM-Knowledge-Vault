# Evidence Handling & Digital Forensics

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Incident Management
> **Topic:** Evidence Handling & Digital Forensics

## Definition

Evidence handling ensures that digital evidence is collected, preserved, and documented so it remains reliable and admissible if needed for legal or disciplinary purposes.

## Why It Matters

Improper handling can compromise investigations, delay recovery, and make evidence unusable.

## CISM Key Points

- Preserve evidence immediately.
- Maintain chain of custody.
- Follow documented procedures.
- Collect only authorized evidence.
- Support investigations without altering evidence.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP explains forensic techniques. CISM focuses on ensuring evidence is handled properly to support business, legal, and regulatory requirements.

## Exam Tip

Always preserve evidence before making unnecessary changes to affected systems.

## Real-World Example

After detecting unauthorized access, the incident response team creates forensic images of affected systems, documents the chain of custody, and securely stores the evidence for investigation.
