# Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Incident Management
> **Topic:** Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

## Definition

Business Continuity (BC) ensures critical business functions continue during a disruption. Disaster Recovery (DR) restores IT systems and data after a disruptive event.

## Why It Matters

Effective BC and DR minimize downtime, protect critical operations, and help the organization recover quickly from incidents.

## CISM Key Points

- BC focuses on business operations.
- DR focuses on IT recovery.
- Recovery priorities come from the Business Impact Analysis (BIA).
- Test BC/DR plans regularly.
- Update plans after major changes or incidents.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP explains BC and DR concepts. CISM emphasizes ensuring recovery plans support business priorities and organizational resilience.

## Exam Tip

Remember: **Business Continuity keeps the business running; Disaster Recovery restores the technology.**

## Real-World Example

After a data center outage, employees continue working from an alternate site while IT restores systems from backups according to the disaster recovery plan.
