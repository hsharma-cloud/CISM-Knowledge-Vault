# Communication & Escalation

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Incident Management
> **Topic:** Communication & Escalation

## Definition

Communication and Escalation ensure that the appropriate people are informed at the right time during a security incident.

## Why It Matters

Clear communication reduces confusion, supports decision-making, and helps minimize business impact.

## CISM Key Points

- Follow the incident communication plan.
- Escalate incidents based on severity.
- Notify the appropriate stakeholders.
- Maintain accurate documentation.
- Protect confidential information.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP focuses on incident handling. CISM emphasizes effective communication with management, legal, regulators, customers, and other stakeholders.

## Exam Tip

Communicate through established procedures. Do not disclose incident details to unauthorized individuals.

## Real-World Example

Following a major data breach, the security manager notifies executive management, legal counsel, and regulatory authorities according to the organization's incident response plan.
