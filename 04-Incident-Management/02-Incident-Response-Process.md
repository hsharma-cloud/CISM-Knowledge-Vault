# Incident Response Process

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Incident Management
> **Topic:** Incident Response Process

## Definition

The Incident Response Process is a structured approach for handling security incidents to minimize business impact and restore normal operations.

## Why It Matters

A defined process enables a fast, consistent, and effective response while protecting critical business assets.

## CISM Key Points

- Preparation
- Detection & Analysis
- Containment
- Eradication
- Recovery
- Lessons Learned

## CISSP Connection

CISSP explains the technical response process. CISM emphasizes coordinating people, communication, and business decisions throughout the incident.

## Exam Tip

Protect the business first. Follow the incident response process and communicate with the appropriate stakeholders.

## Real-World Example

After detecting malware, the incident response team isolates affected systems, removes the malware, restores systems from backups, and documents lessons learned to improve future response.
