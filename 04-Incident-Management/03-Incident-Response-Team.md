# Incident Response Team (IRT)

> **Document Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-04
> **Domain:** Incident Management
> **Topic:** Incident Response Team

## Definition

An Incident Response Team (IRT) is a group of individuals responsible for coordinating and managing security incidents to minimize business impact.

## Why It Matters

A well-defined team with clear roles enables faster response, effective communication, and quicker recovery.

## CISM Key Points

- Define roles and responsibilities.
- Establish communication procedures.
- Coordinate with business stakeholders.
- Escalate incidents appropriately.
- Review performance after each incident.

## CISSP Connection

CISSP focuses on technical response activities. CISM emphasizes leadership, coordination, communication, and business continuity during an incident.

## Exam Tip

Know who should be involved in an incident—not every incident requires executive management, but major incidents should follow the escalation process.

## Real-World Example

A ransomware attack triggers the Incident Response Team, including security, IT, legal, communications, and business leadership, to coordinate containment, recovery, and stakeholder communication.
