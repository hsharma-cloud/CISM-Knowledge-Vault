# Domain 4 Summary – Incident Management

## Goal of Domain 4

Prepare for, respond to, recover from, and learn from security incidents while minimizing business impact.

---

---

## Topics to Remember

### 1. Incident Management Fundamentals
- Prepare before incidents occur.
- Minimize business impact.
- Recover quickly.

### 2. Incident Response Process
- Preparation
- Detection & Analysis
- Containment
- Eradication
- Recovery
- Lessons Learned

### 3. Incident Response Team
- Define roles.
- Coordinate stakeholders.
- Follow escalation procedures.

### 4. Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery
- BC = Business operations.
- DR = IT recovery.
- BIA determines priorities.

### 5. Communication & Escalation
- Follow communication plans.
- Notify the right stakeholders.
- Escalate based on severity.

### 6. Evidence Handling & Digital Forensics
- Preserve evidence.
- Maintain chain of custody.
- Follow documented procedures.

### 7. Post-Incident Review
- Identify root causes.
- Improve the response process.
- Update policies and procedures.

---

# Top CISM Mindset

During an incident, ask:

- What minimizes business impact?
- Have the correct stakeholders been informed?
- Are we following the incident response plan?
- Are we preserving evidence?
- How can we prevent this from happening again?

---

# CISSP vs CISM

CISSP asks:

"How do I respond to the incident?"

CISM asks:

"How do I manage the incident to protect the business?"

---
