# CISM Cheat Sheet (One-Page Review)

## Core Principle

**Security exists to support the business.**

---

## Domain 1 – Governance

- Governance = Direction
- Management = Execution
- Business before Technology
- Security aligns with Business Strategy

Remember:

Policy → Standard → Procedure → Guideline

---

## Domain 2 – Risk

Risk Process:

Identify → Assess → Analyze → Treat → Monitor

Risk Treatment:

- Accept
- Mitigate
- Transfer
- Avoid

Business owns the risk.

---

## Domain 3 – Security Program

- Risk-based
- Business-driven
- Measure performance
- Improve continuously

Remember:

People + Process + Technology

---

## Domain 4 – Incident Management

Prepare

↓

Detect

↓

Respond

↓

Recover

↓

Lessons Learned

Remember:

- Protect the business
- Preserve evidence
- Communicate correctly

---

## Board vs Management

Board

- Governance
- Oversight
- Strategy

Management

- Execute
- Operate
- Implement

---

## BC vs DR

Business Continuity

Business keeps running.

Disaster Recovery

IT systems recover.

---

## KPI vs KRI

KPI

Measures performance.

KRI

Measures risk.

---

## Biggest Exam Rules

✓ Business first

✓ Governance before technology

✓ Business owns risk

✓ Follow the process

✓ Think strategically

✓ Improve continuously

---

## One Sentence to Remember

**CISSP secures technology.**

**CISM manages security to support the business.**
