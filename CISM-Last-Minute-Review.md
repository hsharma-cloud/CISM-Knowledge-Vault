# CISM Last-Minute Review (CISSP → CISM Bridge)

## CISM Mindset

Always think like a manager—not an engineer.

Every decision should:

- Support business objectives
- Reduce business risk
- Deliver business value
- Align with governance
- Be risk-based

---

# Domain 1 – Information Security Governance

Remember:

- Governance provides direction.
- Management executes.
- Security supports the business.
- Align security with business strategy.
- Think business before technology.

Key Frameworks

- COBIT → Governance
- ISO 27001 → ISMS
- NIST CSF → Cybersecurity Risk

Remember

Policy
→ Standard
→ Procedure
→ Guideline

---

# Domain 2 – Risk Management

Risk Lifecycle

Identify
→ Assess
→ Analyze
→ Treat
→ Monitor
→ Report

Risk Treatment

- Accept
- Mitigate
- Transfer
- Avoid

Remember

Business owns the risk.

Security advises.

Management decides.

Risk should be managed—not eliminated.

---

# Domain 3 – Security Program

Security Program

Plan
→ Implement
→ Operate
→ Measure
→ Improve

Remember

- Support business objectives.
- Prioritize by risk.
- Measure performance.
- Continuously improve.

---

# Domain 4 – Incident Management

Incident Lifecycle

Prepare
→ Detect
→ Respond
→ Recover
→ Review
→ Improve

Remember

- Protect the business.
- Preserve evidence.
- Follow procedures.
- Communicate appropriately.
- Learn from every incident.

# CISSP vs CISM

CISSP

How do I secure systems?

CISM

How do I manage security to support the business?

---

# Top Exam Rules

✓ Business first.

✓ Governance before technology.

✓ Risk-based decisions.

✓ Business owns risk.

✓ Security enables the business.

✓ Follow established processes.

✓ Think strategically.

✓ Improve continuously.

---

# If Two Answers Look Correct

Choose the one that:

- Best supports business objectives.
- Aligns with governance.
- Reduces business risk.
- Provides the greatest business value.
- Involves the appropriate management level.

That is usually the CISM answer.
